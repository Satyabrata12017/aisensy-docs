This policy covers the **WhatsApp Notifications via AiSensy** app ("the app") for Frappe / ERPNext, published by Satya (satyabrata12017@gmail.com).

## What the app does with data
The app runs entirely inside your own Frappe site. When a notification you configured is triggered, it sends the following to **AiSensy** (https://aisensy.com) to deliver a WhatsApp template message:

- the recipient's mobile number
- the recipient name (if you configured a Recipient Name Field)
- the template parameter values you mapped from the document
- a link to the media file, if you enabled attachments
- your AiSensy API key, campaign name and user name

## What the publisher collects
**Nothing.** The app has no analytics or tracking, and sends no data to the publisher. All data stays in your site's database and is shared only with AiSensy, as described above.

## Data stored in your site
- **Aisensy Settings:** your AiSensy API key, stored encrypted.
- **Aisensy Notification Log:** the HTTP status and AiSensy's response for each message sent.
- **Aisensy Scheduled Message:** queued scheduled messages.

You can delete these records at any time from your site.

## Third parties
Message delivery is handled by AiSensy and WhatsApp (Meta), under their own privacy policies:
- AiSensy: https://aisensy.com/privacy-policy
- WhatsApp: https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy

You are responsible for having your customers' consent to receive WhatsApp messages, and for following WhatsApp Business policies and applicable law (e.g. GDPR, India's DPDP Act).
